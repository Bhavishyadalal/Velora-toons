Your space

Only the API address belongs here. Never paste passwords, tokens, or signed media URLs. This address is saved on your device.

Keyboard: / search · Esc close dialogs · built-in video only: Space play/pause, ←/→ seek, M mute, F fullscreen. Provider players use their own shortcuts.

A little behind the magic.

Velora Toons reads public catalogue and player metadata through your backend. Video streams from its original provider to your browser; Render does not download or relay the video.

Availability, language options, ads, and embedding permissions belong to the source. Removed videos cannot be restored here. Downloads appear only when an adapter provides a verified media file URL.

Your favourites, settings, and optional recent history stay in this browser. No Velora account is required.
